# India Zoho Books / Deluge / API developer and consultant rates (hourly, daily, monthly)

Note: figures come from search-result summaries and fetched pages (Aug-Jul 2026 pages where dated). AmbitionBox direct fetch returned 403, so salary figures are from search snippets only. Most rate pages are vendor marketing.

## What are typical hourly and daily rates (INR/USD) by level and platform?

### Takeaway
Published Indian freelancer rates cluster around $8-25/hr (about INR 400-2,100/hr); agencies/partners quote $20-50/hr; partner day rates are stated at INR 3,500-12,000. No source gives rates cleanly split junior/mid/senior.

### Cited Findings
- India Zoho profile on Freelancer lists $25/hr; a Pune Zoho developer on Hubstaff Talent lists $8/hr with 1 year experience — [Freelancer.pk](https://www.freelancer.pk/freelancers/skills/zoho), [Hubstaff Talent](https://hubstafftalent.net/profiles/ayesha-karim-mujawar)
- A Freelancer Zoho Creator project was listed at INR 400-750/hr, with bids averaging about INR 808/hr — [Freelancer](https://www.freelancer.com.au/projects/zoho-creator/zoho-creator-modification-development/)
- An Indian Zoho Inventory/Books consultant accepted a client rate of INR 400/hr on a single project — [Freelancer.de](https://www.freelancer.de/projects/inventory-management/expert-zoho-inventory-books)
- Upwork job posting (Indian client, Zoho CRM/Creator setup and integration) budget $15-40/hr — [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Zoho-CRM-Setup-and-Integration_~022089246669095818894/)
- Flexiple models a Zoho consultant freelance contractor at about $9/hr (160 hrs/month, 2x multiplier; contractor median about $10/hr), explicitly "market rate, not pricing"; page dated Aug 2026; no level split — [Flexiple](https://flexiple.com/hire/zoho)
- Fiverr India seller offers Deluge script work at $50 per gig (fixed price, about a week delivery) — [Fiverr](https://fiverr.com/tonmoymuhury/write-deluge-script-in-zoho)
- An India-based general full-stack developer on Freelancer lists $12/hr (not Zoho-specific) — [Freelancer.in](https://www.freelancer.in/freelancers/skills/zoho-creator)
- Indian Zoho partners on time-and-materials: INR 3,500-12,000 per consultant day (excl. GST), by seniority and partner tier, no per-tier split given (Aaxonix, vendor content, dated 20 Jul 2026) — [Aaxonix](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-implementation-partner-cost-india/)
- Community discussions cited in the same article put integration work at roughly $60-90/hr (not partner rates, not India-specific); one owner's quote about $9,000 for 130 hours (about $69/hr) — [Aaxonix](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-implementation-partner-cost-india/)

### Inferences
- INR 3,500-12,000/day at 8 hrs is about INR 440-1,500/hr (about $5-18/hr), well below the $20-50/hr agency quotes below; the day-rate range likely reflects small/regional partners. This is my arithmetic, not a published figure.
- Plausible freelancer tiers (inference only): junior about $8-12/hr, mid about $12-25/hr, senior/specialist about $25-40/hr. Evidence is thin scattered listings.

### Gaps
- No Upwork/Toptal India Zoho profile data with job-success or earnings stats was retrievable; no clean junior/mid/senior split.

- Retainers: INR 15,000/month basic support; INR 60,000+/month active development/multi-product — [Aaxonix](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-implementation-partner-cost-india/)
- Zoho Books fixed-fee implementation (partner fee only, excl. licence/GST 18%): small (to 25 users) INR 40,000-90,000; mid (25-100) INR 90,000-2,00,000; enterprise INR 2,00,000-5,00,000+. ### Gaps

## Monthly salary ranges (cost-per-day cross-check)

### Takeaway
Zoho developer salaries in India are roughly INR 3.6-10 lakh/yr for junior-mid (about INR 30,000-85,000/month); senior up to about INR 15 lakh+. Note some data is for employees of Zoho Corp, not ecosystem developers.

### Cited Findings
- AmbitionBox, Zoho developer (20+ submissions): INR 4.3-10 lakh/yr, 0-4 years; take-home about INR 53,000-54,000/month — [AmbitionBox](https://www.ambitionbox.com/salaries/zoho-salaries/developer) (search snippet; page not directly fetchable); note this is Zoho Corporation employees
- AmbitionBox, Zoho software developer: INR 5-20 lakh/yr (under 1 to 5 years) — [AmbitionBox](https://www.ambitionbox.com/salaries/zoho-salaries/software-developer)
- Flexiple: median Zoho pay in India from INR 5.4 lakh (0-2 yrs) to INR 14 lakh (12+ yrs) — [Flexiple](https://flexiple.com/salary/zoho/mumbai)
- Zoho CRM developer job, Ahmedabad, 3+ years: INR 3.6-7.6 lakh/yr — [Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/jobs/81977)
- Senior Zoho Developer posting, 4-6 yrs: INR 6-10 lakh/yr — [Erekrut](https://erekrut.com/?p=7746316)
- Zoho CRM & Creator contract developer, 2-4 yrs: INR 3-5 lakh/yr — [Indeed](https://in.indeed.com/Zoho-Crm-Developer-jobs)
- Surat posting, 2 yrs: INR 15,000-50,000/month — [TeamLease](https://teamlease.com/jobs/zoho-developer-recruitment-in-surat-1789725)
- Jooble estimates about INR 49,745/month India average — [Jooble](https://in.jooble.org/salary/zoho-developer/Mumbai)

### Inferences
- INR 6-10 lakh/yr is about INR 50,000-83,000/month, about INR 2,300-3,800 per working day of salary cost (22 days). Freelance/agency rates of INR 3,500-12,000/day imply roughly 1.5-3x salary-cost markup (my arithmetic).

### Gaps
- No Glassdoor data; no Zoho Books-specific salary page.
