# India pricing for custom MIS / financial reporting and dashboard development on Zoho Books (and similar)

Method note: 10 searches and fetches were run. Pages for aaxonix (implementation cost) and cloudfysystems were fetched in full. The rest are search-result summaries only. Almost all figures are vendor or agency marketing, or freelancer bids, not audited market data. No source priced P&L / Balance Sheet / Trial Balance MIS reports, cash flow forecasting, COGS/margin reports or Zoho Books web-tab portals specifically. Today is 2026-10-14.

## 1. Typical per-report, per-dashboard and per-project prices (INR) for MIS/BI

### Takeaway
Published ranges: roughly INR 40k-2.5 lakh for a Zoho Analytics SMB rollout (about 5-10 dashboards), INR 1 lakh and up for small Power BI dashboard projects, and about INR 6k-20k per month for freelancer Tally/Excel MIS packs. No per-report (P&L/BS/TB) list prices were found.

### Cited Findings
- Zoho Analytics partner implementation fees (Jul 2026, excl. 18% GST): small (up to 25 users) INR 40,000-90,000; mid-size (25-100 users) INR 90,000-2,00,000; enterprise INR 2,00,000-5,00,000+. Zoho Creator custom app: INR 80,000-2,00,000 (small), 2-5 lakh (mid), 5-15 lakh+ (enterprise). Each custom integration INR 30,000-1,50,000. Extra training sessions INR 8,000-20,000. — [Aaxonix, Zoho implementation partner cost India](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-implementation-partner-cost-india/)
- A single-department SMB Zoho Analytics deployment (2-4 data sources, 5-10 dashboards) is typically INR 60,000-2.5 lakh. Multi-department work with row-level security and embedded analytics is about INR 3-8 lakh. The source calls these typical ranges, not quotes, and says data-modelling time drives the tiers. — [Aaxonix, Zoho Analytics implementation partner](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-analytics-implementation-partner/)
- PrecisionTech (Authorized Zoho Partner) lists one-time Zoho Analytics implementation packages of INR 5,999 (Starter), 12,999 (Standard) and 24,999 (Professional). Subscription is billed separately. Scope of each package was not verified. — [PrecisionTech Zoho Analytics](https://precisiontech.in/software/zoho/zoho-analytics/)
- Power BI: fixed-price projects from INR 1,00,000 for small dashboards to INR 20,00,000+ for complex enterprise BI, described as indicative. A dedicated Power BI team costs INR 1,50,000-6,00,000 per month. — [Acquaintsoft FAQ](https://acquaintsoft.com/faqs/how-much-does-it-cost-to-hire-a-power-bi-expert-from-india)
- A BeyondKey guide estimates a starter dashboard at about 80-100 offshore hours, priced at USD 3,200-4,000 (not India-specific, USD). — [BeyondKey Power BI consulting cost](https://www.beyondkey.com/blog/power-bi-consulting-cost/)
- Freelancer.in bids for dashboard work run about INR 1,000-25,000 (individual bids, low end unrealistic). — [Freelancer.in Power BI](https://www.freelancer.in/projects/power-bi/goa-power-dashboard-specialist)
- Excel automation / MIS dashboards listing: average of 10 bids INR 20,490, with one offer of about INR 12,500 for 2-day delivery. — [Freelancer, reporting automation](https://www.freelancer.com.au/projects/automation/advanced-data-reporting-automation)
- Ongoing Tally accounting support with monthly MIS: average of 15 bids INR 20,091. Smaller Tally tasks: average bids of about INR 6,300-6,700. — [Freelancer.in, ongoing Tally support](https://www.freelancer.in/projects/bank-reconciliation/ongoing-tally-accounting-support); [Freelancer, Tally Prime assistance](https://www.freelancer.de/projects/data-entry/tally-prime-accounting-assistance)
- Zoho Books standard implementation (COA, GST, migration, training) is typically INR 15,000-50,000, which gives a baseline for the non-reporting setup. — [Patron Accounting, Zoho Books pricing India 2026](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/zoho-books-pricing-india-2026)

### Inferences
- A bespoke financial MIS pack (P&L, BS, TB, cash flow) would plausibly sit around the Zoho Analytics SMB band (INR 60k-2.5 lakh) or the small Power BI band (INR 1 lakh and up). This is not directly sourced.
- Because freelancer bids (INR 6k-25k) are far below agency figures, a solo developer could plausibly charge INR 15k-60k per report set. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- No published price list for individual P&L / Balance Sheet / Trial Balance report builds, margin/COGS reports, or cash flow forecasting tools in India.
- Aaxonix and similar partner blogs are marketing content, and their ranges are not backed by disclosed project data.

## 2. Hourly and day rates of Indian BI/MIS developers and consultants

### Takeaway
Quoted India rates are about INR 1,200-3,500 per hour for Power BI via agencies, INR 750-2,500 per hour for freelancers, and INR 3,500-12,000 per day for Zoho partner consultants. Salary data implies INR 4-7 lakh per year for typical Power BI developers.

### Cited Findings
- Power BI hourly INR 1,200-3,500 (Indian vendor, indicative). — [Acquaintsoft](https://acquaintsoft.com/faqs/how-much-does-it-cost-to-hire-a-power-bi-expert-from-india)
- Freelancer.de bid of INR 2,000-2,500 per hour for a Power BI project; another provider quoted INR 1,500 for a one-hour consult (about four years old). — [Freelancer.de](https://www.freelancer.de/projects/power-bi/power-developer-analyst-for-reput); [Freelancer.de](https://www.freelancer.de/projects/power-bi/support-for-power-developer-role)
- Flexiple modelled contractor rates (not quotes): Power BI Bengaluru about USD 11/hr, Chennai about USD 7.5/hr. — [Flexiple Bengaluru](https://flexiple.com/hire/microsoft-power-bi/bengaluru); [Flexiple Chennai](https://flexiple.com/hire/microsoft-power-bi/chennai)
- Zoho partner consultant day rates INR 3,500-12,000 (Jul 2026). — [Aaxonix](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-implementation-partner-cost-india/)
- Zoho setup freelancer bids INR 750-1,250 per hour; one closed posting at INR 400 per hour. — [Freelancer.de zoho books setup](https://www.freelancer.de/projects/zoho-creator/zoho-books-setup)
- Zoho retainers from INR 15,000 per month (basic support) up to INR 60,000+ per month (active development). — [Aaxonix](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-implementation-partner-cost-india/)
- AmbitionBox employer-level Power BI developer pay: Capita INR 3.5-6.1 lakh per year; MindTrail 3.5-6.2 lakh (2-4 yrs); Xcaliber 6-9.5 lakh (8 reports); Fractal about 4 lakh at 2 yrs to 8-19 lakh at 5 yrs. Some pages are low-confidence or dated 2023. — [Capita](https://www.ambitionbox.com/salaries/capita-salaries/power-bi-developer); [Fractal](https://www.ambitionbox.com/salaries/fractal-analytics-salaries/power-bi-developer); [Xcaliber](https://www.ambitionbox.com/salaries/xcaliber-infotech-salaries/power-bi-developer); [MindTrail](https://www.ambitionbox.com/salaries/mindtrail-technologies-salaries/power-bi-developer)

### Inferences
- A mid-level in-house BI developer at about INR 6 lakh per year costs roughly INR 300-400 per hour in salary terms, so agency and freelancer rates carry a 3-8x markup. This is arithmetic, not sourced.
- INR 1,500-2,500 per hour is a reasonable independent senior freelancer band (small sample).

### Gaps
- No Zoho Analytics-specific hourly rate from a primary India source. No Upwork/UrbanPro/Sulekha/IndiaMART listings were retrieved. No aggregate AmbitionBox or Glassdoor Power BI figure was retrieved.

## 3. Zoho Analytics implementation/consulting costs and licence costs (INR)

### Takeaway
Licences start at about INR 960 per month and the plans are cheap. Sources conflict on whether pricing is per user or per organisation, so confirm on Zoho's own pricing page. Partner implementation is INR 40k-5 lakh+ by size.

### Cited Findings
- Per-organisation, annual billing, plus 18% GST (claimed March 2026): Basic (2 users) INR 960 per month, Standard (5 users) INR 1,900, Premium (15 users) INR 4,200, extra users INR 375 each per month. — [Codroid IT Labs](https://codroiditlabs.com/zoho-analytics-partner-india/)
- Another partner (2026): INR 960 / 1,920 / 4,800 / 9,600+ per month for Basic / Standard / Premium / Enterprise (50 users). — [ITforSME](https://itforsme.in/pricing/zoho-analytics-india)
- Older/per-user claim: Basic INR 840 and Standard INR 2,100 per user per month. — [PrecisionTech](https://precisiontech.in/software/zoho/zoho-analytics/)
- April 2025 data: Basic INR 24,000, Standard 60,000, Premium 1,44,000 per year (dated, unclear sourcing). — [Aaxonix](https://aaxonix.com/resources/?p=3480)
- Zoho One: INR 1,250 per user per month (All Employee plan, annual) per one partner; another source says INR 2,500-3,500 per user per month. These conflict. — [Codroid](https://codroiditlabs.com/zoho-analytics-partner-india/); [Aaxonix](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-implementation-partner-cost-india/)
- Implementation fees: see Section 1.

### Inferences
- Licence cost is small relative to build cost for an SMB (under INR 1 lakh per year).

### Gaps
- Zoho's official India pricing page was not fetched, so the licence figures above are reseller-reported and conflicting.

## 4. Custom Python/web (Flask/FastAPI/MariaDB) portals, cash flow forecasting and inventory COGS automation

### Takeaway
India custom web app/dashboard/portal builds are quoted at INR 2-30 lakh by agencies, with developer rates of about USD 9-35 per hour (INR 800-3,000 per hour). No source priced cash flow forecasting or COGS automation specifically, or Zoho Books web-tab embeds.

### Cited Findings
- Custom web application (internal tools, dashboards, portals) INR 5-30 lakh in 2026; simpler CRM-integrated business app INR 1-2 lakh. — [Ecorpit](https://ecorpit.com/ecorpit-custom-web-application-development-india-2026/)
- Another guide: INR 2-15 lakh+ for a custom web application. — [Redefineweb](https://redefineweb.com/blog/custom-web-portal-development-services-india-real-tiers/)
- Hosting INR 2,000-25,000 per month; annual maintenance 15-25% of build cost; first-year total 25-30% above the quote. — [Ecorpit](https://ecorpit.com/ecorpit-custom-web-application-development-india-2026/); [Redefineweb](https://redefineweb.com/blog/custom-web-portal-development-services-india-real-tiers/)
- Flexiple modelled FastAPI contractor rates: Hyderabad mid-level USD 18/hr (range 11-34); Mumbai USD 16/hr (9.5-24). A vendor claims Indian teams charge USD 15-35/hr. — [Flexiple FastAPI Hyderabad](https://flexiple.com/salary/fastapi/hyderabad); [Flexiple Mumbai](https://flexiple.com/salary/fastapi/mumbai)
- Glassdoor Flask developer: INR 7 lakh per year (about INR 337/hr), from only 2 submissions. — [Glassdoor India](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Salaries/australia-python-flask-developer-salary-SRCH_IL.0,9_KO10,32.htm)
- Zoho custom integration INR 30,000-1,50,000 each. — [Aaxonix](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-implementation-partner-cost-india/)

### Inferences
- A scoped Zoho Books-connected report portal (Flask/MariaDB, a few financial reports) falls between the "CRM-integrated app" (INR 1-2 lakh) and low-end custom web app (INR 2-5 lakh) bands, with cash flow forecasting and COGS logic adding to that. Not directly sourced.
- At the Flexiple and agency rates, 150-300 hours would be roughly INR 2-9 lakh. Arithmetic only.

### Gaps
- No sources found for cash flow forecasting tools, inventory COGS/margin automation, or Zoho Books web-tab portals in India. No Clutch/GoodFirms data was retrieved.
