# Pricing 11.5 Zoho Books days in India

**No Indian source publishes a rate for any of the ten work types you listed, so a quote has to be built from generic Zoho-partner day rates and benchmark inferences.** The published anchors are: **INR 3,500-12,000 per consultant day** at Zoho partners (ex-GST) ([Aaxonix](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-implementation-partner-cost-india/)); **USD 20-50/hr** at Indian agencies ([RaftLabs](https://www.raftlabs.com/blog/top-zoho-development-companies)); about **INR 750-2,500/hr** for freelancers ([Freelancer.de](https://www.freelancer.de/projects/zoho-creator/zoho-books-setup)); and **INR 30,000-1,50,000 per custom integration** ([Aaxonix](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-implementation-partner-cost-india/)). Multiplying 11.5 days by those day rates gives roughly **INR 40,000-1.4 lakh at partner day rates, INR 0.7-2.3 lakh at freelancer rates, and INR 1.6-3.9 lakh at agency USD rates** (my arithmetic; USD converted at an assumed ~INR 85). A defensible quote for a senior, project-specific build sits around **INR 1.5-2.5 lakh ex-GST** (my judgment, not a published figure). Add 18% GST, expect 2% TDS on payment, and price support separately at roughly 15-25% of build per year. Almost every source is vendor marketing, so treat all ranges as soft. One gap: the project's 16 line items were not in the notes or the repo files I searched, so the mapping below is by your ten work categories, with an assumed split of the 11.5 days.

## Published rates: day, hour and monthly

Indian Zoho consulting is quoted in three tiers that barely overlap. Partners sell **INR 3,500-12,000 per consultant day** by seniority and tier, with retainers from **INR 15,000/month** (basic support) to **INR 60,000+/month** (active development) ([Aaxonix](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-implementation-partner-cost-india/)). Agencies publish hourly USD rates: Encaptechno and TransFunnel about **$20-45**, RaftLabs **$29-49** ([RaftLabs](https://www.raftlabs.com/blog/top-zoho-development-companies)), 10xBiz **$25-49** with a $1,000+ minimum ([Clutch](https://clutch.co/profile/10xbiz)), and ERPOcean about **$50** ([DesignRush](https://www.designrush.com/agency/profile/erpocean)). Freelancers sit lower: Zoho Books setup bids around **INR 750-1,250/hr**, one closed job at INR 400/hr ([Freelancer.de](https://www.freelancer.de/projects/zoho-creator/zoho-books-setup)), an average of about **INR 1,036/hr** on another ([Freelancer.in](https://www.freelancer.in/projects/zoho-creator/zoho-books-setup-40441969)), listed profiles at **$8-25/hr** ([Hubstaff Talent](https://hubstafftalent.net/profiles/ayesha-karim-mujawar), [Freelancer.pk](https://www.freelancer.pk/freelancers/skills/zoho)), and a modelled contractor median near **$9-10/hr** ([Flexiple](https://flexiple.com/hire/zoho)).

Python and API work is not separately priced anywhere I could find. A community-reported **$60-90/hr** for integration work (not India-specific) ([Aaxonix](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-implementation-partner-cost-india/)) and modelled FastAPI contractor rates of **$16-18/hr** for Mumbai and Hyderabad ([Flexiple](https://flexiple.com/salary/fastapi/hyderabad)) bracket the plausible range. Salary data gives the floor: Zoho developers earn roughly INR 3.6-10 lakh a year ([Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/jobs/81977), [Erekrut](https://erekrut.com/?p=7746316)), which is about INR 2,300-3,800 per working day in salary cost (my arithmetic), so partner day rates carry a 1.5-3x markup. Treat the **INR 3,500-12,000 day range as the low end** for a full-stack Python plus Deluge build; it reflects configuration consultants, and the agency USD rates convert to roughly INR 13,600-34,000 a day (my arithmetic).

## Support, GST, TDS and payment terms

On support, partners publish retainers of **INR 15,000-40,000/month** ([RaftLabs](https://www.raftlabs.com/blog/top-zoho-development-companies)) and Digiwah **INR 25,000-1,00,000/month** with 30 days of hypercare ([Digiwah](https://digiwah.com/zoho/)); Codroid sells yearly packs at **INR 20,000-50,000** (basic) and INR 50,000-1,50,000 (priority SLA) ([Codroid Labs](https://codroiditlabs.com/how-much-does-zoho-crm-implementation-cost-in-india/)). No Indian partner publishes AMC as a percentage; the nearest figures are **15-20%** a year in government software contracts (a secondary summary) ([echai.ventures](https://echai.ventures/gtm/from/tenderdekho)) and **15-25%** in general benchmarks ([Edana](https://edana.ch/?p=44898)). Zoho's own Books Premium support is the higher of INR 40,000/year or 20% of subscription ([Zoho](https://zstatic.zohostatic.com/in/books/support-pricing/)), a vendor add-on and not a customisation rate. Applying 15-25% to a INR 2 lakh build gives INR 30,000-50,000 a year (inference).

Domestic custom software is **18% GST**, most often under SAC 998314, although sources disagree on SAC mapping for hosting (998314 vs 998315) ([Xflowpay](https://www.xflowpay.com/blog/gst-on-software-services), [Tally Solutions](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/gst-software-saas-exports-international-clients/)). Quote ex-GST. Zoho partner fees are likewise stated ex-GST. Exports are zero-rated under LUT only if all s.2(6) IGST conditions hold, which does not matter for an Indian client. For TDS, fees for technical services under 194J have been **2%** since 1 April 2020, while contract-for-work under 194C is **1% (individual/HUF) or 2% (others)** ([Taxbuddy](https://www.taxbuddy.com/blog/tds-194c-vs-194j-what-s-the-difference)); a commonly cited annual threshold under 194J is INR 30,000, with 20% where PAN is missing ([BankBazaar](https://11estates.bankbazaar.com/tax/section-194j-tds-on-professional-technical-fee.html)). Software-development classification is arguable and sources are secondary, so have a CA confirm. For payment terms, a private vendor schedule is **20% advance, then 30%, 40% and 10%** ([Prakity](https://prakity.com/payment-terms)); government tenders use 10-25% advances ([Odisha RFP](https://odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-02/Corrigendum-03_13.02.2024_RFP-24015.pdf)); Zoho partners milestone fixed fees across configuration, migration, training and go-live ([Aaxonix](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-implementation-partner-cost-india/)). No source confirmed 50% advance as a norm. Quote validity in Indian tenders runs **30-90 days** ([IIT Kanpur](https://iitk.ac.in/new/hindi/data/tender/file/IITK_DESIGN_VCL_2013-14_003.pdf), [ICFRE](https://www.icfre.org/tender/tender85.pdf)); 30 days for a private quote is my inference.

## Conclusion

The practical finding is that this project's value is in the combination (a Books-API data layer, a computed-COGS Deluge function with back-fill, and a hosted portal) that no Indian price list covers, so the quote cannot be anchored to a published rate for the whole. Day-rate arithmetic produces a number (INR 40,000-1.7 lakh) that reads as a configuration job. The published fixed-price anchors for the Deluge, integration and portal components alone point to INR 2-4 lakh, which gives you room to defend INR 1.5-2.5 lakh without appearing high.

To tighten the figure, map your actual 16 line items to the table's rows and replace my day allocation with your own. Then decide whether to state the price as fixed, which the sources favour for tight scope, or as T&M with a cap. Add support as a separate line, since sources agree it is priced apart from the build, and keep every price ex-GST with 20-30% advance and milestone billing.
